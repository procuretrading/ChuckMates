Will Chuck Mates® Fit My Vehicle?

Home » Blog » Will Chuck Mates® Fit My Vehicle?

If your tires have Schrader valves, yes. Chuck Mates® fit standard Schrader valve stems, the 8V1-thread valve used on cars, trucks, RVs, and motorcycles. The exception you are most likely to run into is the Presta valve, which is standard on road bikes and most performance mountain bikes. Chuck Mates don’t fit Presta directly.

Will Chuck Mates fit your vehicle?

Yes, with one main outlier worth mentioning. Chuck Mates® fit all standard Schrader valve stems, which is the common valve stem “used on virtually every motor vehicle in the world today,” according to Schrader. The technical specification for Schrader is 8V1 thread, 0.305"-32 TPI, and it is also called American valve (AV). That covers the valve stems on cars, trucks, RVs, and motorcycles. The outlier is a specialty valve called the Presta valve, which is standard on road bikes and most performance mountain bikes.

What is a Schrader valve?

A Schrader valve is a tire valve with a threaded metal stem and a spring-loaded valve core inside. Air flows only while the core’s center pin is pressed in, which is exactly what an inflator chuck does when you connect it.

The outside of the stem carries the 8V1 thread. That thread is what your valve cap screws onto, what a screw-on inflator chuck grips, and what a Chuck Mate is built to fit. SKOG’s FAQ puts it plainly: Chuck Mates work on “all standard tire valve stems,” meaning the Schrader type.

How can you tell a Schrader valve from a Presta valve?

A Schrader valve looks like the valve on a car tire: a wide stem with a pin recessed inside the threaded end. A Presta valve is thinner and has a small knurled nut at the tip that you unscrew before adding air.

The quick test: if a gas-station air hose fits it, it is a Schrader valve. Michelin notes that Schrader valves are the same format as car tire valves and work with gas-station compressors.

Bikes have a third type too. The Dunlop valve, also called the Woods valve, is still widely used in parts of Europe and Asia, per Michelin. It is not a Schrader valve either.

Do Chuck Mates fit cars, trucks, RVs, and motorcycles?

Yes, as long as the tires use standard Schrader valves, which is the norm on those vehicles. SKOG’s Amazon listing for Chuck Mates® Platinum names cars, bikes, motorcycles, RVs, trucks, ATVs, and UTVs: “anything with a standard Schrader valve.”

Fitting the thread and reaching the stem are two different problems. On dual rear wheel trucks, the inner valve stems can be hard to get to. SKOG’s FAQ recommends the Reach360 7-inch extension for duallies with “blind” valve stems, listed on the Platinum Series page.

Who was Schrader, and why is the valve named after him?

The Schrader valve is named after August Schrader and his son George. August was a German-American who made fittings and valves for rubber goods in New York, and George patented the valve in 1893.

August Schrader (1807–1894) opened his Manhattan shop in the mid-1840s, making valves and fittings for rubber products such as air pillows and life preservers. When pneumatic bicycle tires took off around 1890, he saw the need for a proper tire valve. His son George did much of the development work. The first valves were made in 1891, and U.S. Patent No. 495,064 was issued to George H. F. Schrader on April 11, 1893.

A valve cap came next, and in 1898 a replaceable valve core, the same kind that sits inside nearly every car tire valve today. So when automobiles arrived, a proven, serviceable design was already waiting. By the early 1900s it was the go-to valve on car and truck tires, and more than a century later it’s still the long-standing standard.

FAQ

Do Chuck Mates fit Presta valves?

Not directly. Chuck Mates® are built for the Schrader (8V1) thread, so a Presta valve would need a Presta-to-Schrader adapter.

Is a Schrader valve the same as an American valve?

Yes. “American valve” (AV) is another name for the Schrader valve.

Will Chuck Mates work with my portable inflator?

Per SKOG’s FAQ, yes: if your inflator’s chuck screws onto a tire valve stem, you can screw it onto a Chuck Mate instead.

Can Chuck Mates reach the inner tires on a dually?

SKOG recommends the Reach360 7-inch extension, which it says is designed specifically for duallies with “blind” valve stems.

How do I know if my bike has Schrader valves?

If the valve looks like a car tire valve and a gas-station air hose fits it, it is Schrader. A thin valve with a small nut at the tip is Presta.
